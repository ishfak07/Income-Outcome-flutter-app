Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your Pocketly account

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your Pocketly account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request using the button below, preferably from the email address registered with Pocketly.
State clearly that you want your Pocketly account and associated data permanently deleted.
Pocketly may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing the deletion.
After verification, the account and associated Pocketly data covered by the request will be deleted, except information that must legitimately be retained.
Do not send your password, PIN, bank details, or full transaction history. They are not needed to request account deletion.
Request account deletion by email

The publisher must configure the support email before this page is used in production.

Data covered by the deletion request

  • Firebase Authentication account;
  • Pocketly user profile information;
  • income and expense records associated with the account;
  • monthly budget and other account-associated finance data;
  • uploaded profile photo, if one exists;
  • other Pocketly data directly associated with the deleted account.

Retention

If specific information must be retained for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reason, only the information required for that purpose should be retained for the applicable period and described in Pocketly's Privacy Policy.

Deletion is permanent

After deletion has been completed, deleted account information normally cannot be restored. Temporarily disabling or freezing an account is not treated as account deletion.
