1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pocketly mobile application published by IR TECH and to information processed through the app's related services.

Pocketly is currently intended for users in Sri Lanka. This policy applies whenever you use Pocketly or contact IR TECH about Pocketly.

2. Information we collect

Pocketly collects information that is necessary to create and manage an account and to provide personal finance tracking features.

Category Examples Why it is used
Account information Name, email address, Firebase user ID Account creation, authentication, account management, and linking your data to your account
Optional profile information Phone number, bio, selected emoji/avatar Profile personalization and account functionality
Financial records you enter Income, expenses, amounts, categories, descriptions, dates, times, payment methods, recurring settings, and monthly budget To provide Pocketly's finance tracking, budgeting, history, statistics, reports, and related features
App preferences Currency, theme, and related settings To remember your selected preferences and personalize the app experience
Authentication and technical data Email authentication details and technical information processed by Firebase Authentication, such as IP address and user-agent information Authentication, security, fraud prevention, and operation of the authentication service

Pocketly does not require a profile photo upload. Profile personalization uses selectable emoji/avatar options.

Your account password is handled through Firebase Authentication. IR TECH does not receive your password in plain text.

3. How we use information

IR TECH uses information processed through Pocketly to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage your Pocketly account;
  • store and display the income, expense, budget, and transaction information you enter;
  • calculate summaries, statistics, reports, and financial-health information;
  • save your currency, theme, profile, and app preferences;
  • support password reset, account security, and email verification;
  • provide optional local PIN or biometric quick-access features;
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests;
  • protect Pocketly, its users, and its services from misuse or security threats; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.
Pocketly is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide banking services, payment processing, investment services, lending, or financial advice.

4. Firebase and service providers

Pocketly uses Google Firebase services to provide core application functionality. These services may process information on behalf of IR TECH.

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used for account registration, login, email verification, password reset, and account management.

Cloud Firestore

Cloud Firestore is used to store Pocketly account and finance-related data, including user profile information, income records, expense records, and account-associated settings such as monthly budget.

Google/Firebase may process technical information necessary to operate these services. Their processing is governed by Google's and Firebase's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. How information is shared

IR TECH does not sell Pocketly user data and does not share Pocketly user data with advertisers or marketing companies.

Information may be disclosed only in limited circumstances such as:

  • Service providers: Google/Firebase processes data as needed to provide authentication, database, hosting/infrastructure, and related application services.
  • User-directed actions: information is transferred when you intentionally export or share a file through your device.
  • Legal and security reasons: information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, enforce rights, investigate abuse, or protect users and services.

Pocketly currently does not contain advertising and does not use user data for targeted advertising.

6. Local PIN and biometric security

Pocketly may offer optional PIN and biometric quick-access features on supported devices.

Biometric verification is handled by your device's operating system and biometric security framework. IR TECH does not receive or store your fingerprint, face scan, or biometric template.

If you enable quick login, authentication-related information needed for that feature may be stored locally using platform secure-storage mechanisms on your device.

7. CSV and PDF exports

Pocketly allows you to export selected financial records to CSV or PDF. Export and sharing are initiated by you.

When you choose to share an exported file, your device's operating system may present third-party apps or services through the system share sheet. Information sent to a third-party app at your direction is then handled according to that third party's privacy practices.

Review the destination before sharing exported financial information.

8. Data retention

Pocketly account data is generally retained while your account remains active and as needed to provide the app's functionality.

After IR TECH receives and verifies a valid account-deletion request, IR TECH will aim to complete deletion of the account and associated Pocketly data within 30 days.

Certain limited information may be retained beyond this period only when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security and integrity of Pocketly.

9. Account and data deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your Pocketly account and associated data from within the Pocketly app or through the public account-deletion page.

You may be asked to verify ownership of the account before deletion is completed. Do not send your password, PIN, bank details, or full transaction history when requesting deletion.

A verified deletion request is intended to remove the Pocketly account and account-associated data, including the Firebase Authentication account, user profile, income records, expense records, monthly budget, and other Pocketly data directly associated with that account, subject to the limited retention circumstances described above.

10. Security

IR TECH takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Pocketly information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, and disclosure.

Pocketly uses Firebase services for core backend functionality. Firebase states that its services use HTTPS for data in transit and that services including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore encrypt data at rest.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their account credentials, device access, PIN, and email account.

11. International data processing

Although Pocketly is intended for users in Sri Lanka, Google/Firebase services may process or store information outside Sri Lanka.

Firebase Authentication is operated using infrastructure in the United States, while other Firebase services may use Google infrastructure in different locations depending on the service and configuration.

By using Pocketly, your information may therefore be processed in countries other than Sri Lanka where service providers operate their infrastructure.

12. Age requirements

Pocketly is intended for users aged 13 years and older. It is not intended for children under 13.

If IR TECH becomes aware that personal information belonging to a child under 13 has been provided through Pocketly contrary to this age requirement, IR TECH will take reasonable steps to investigate and delete the information where appropriate.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the nature of your request, you may contact IR TECH to:

  • ask questions about how Pocketly handles your information;
  • request correction of inaccurate profile information;
  • request access to information associated with your account;
  • request deletion of your Pocketly account and associated data;
  • stop using optional profile information, such as your phone number or bio; and
  • raise a privacy concern or complaint.

IR TECH may need to verify your identity or account ownership before acting on a request involving account data.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

IR TECH may update this Privacy Policy when Pocketly's features, data practices, legal requirements, or service providers change.

When this policy is updated, the effective date shown at the top of this page will be revised. Material changes may also be communicated through Pocketly or other reasonable means where appropriate.
